# MetroGo + Northstar visual design
The design borrows the broad hero / category navigation / collection layout idea from the user-provided MilkyBloom reference. No MilkyBloom brand assets, source code, or product data are used.
Palette: forest #183638, teal #24715d, coral #ed7f63, butter #f5d774, lilac #e9dffd, cream #fff9ef.
The two original AI-generated 3D artworks are saved in Assignment1/assets and Assignment2/src/AgentPortal/wwwroot/media. The full prompts are recorded in image-prompts.md. They are illustrations, not product photographs.
Real photographs are bundled locally for offline demos. Source pages, download URLs, and license links are recorded in photo-sources.json.
30-second clips use animated camera movement and three timed captions over the original 3D artwork. They are silent motion graphics, not live-action footage or externally generated character-animation video. MP4 is used in the web portal, and GIF in the WinForms applications. Pause/resume and reduced-motion handling are implemented.
Editable Figma handoff: [Northstar Labs 1 + 2 UI](https://www.figma.com/design/XJaFLoJzp8NrexLNmOHBAk). It includes editable native frames for the MetroGo ticket machine, Northstar partner login and Northstar administrator sign-in. A captured login page is kept as a layout reference; the temporary app capture script was removed after capture.
